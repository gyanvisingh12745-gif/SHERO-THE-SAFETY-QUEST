# SHERO: The Safety Quest — Complete 15-Chapter Saga

> **"Every choice shapes your journey from the streets to self-determination."**

A finished, premium, **comic-book + anime**, **lag-free**, full-screen browser game that teaches personal street safety, cyber defense, relationship red flags, domestic boundary enforcement, and legal rights through an interactive narrative.

---

## 🌟 The 15 Connected Chapters

### Part A: Street Smarts (Campus & Public Safety)
1. **A1 — The Last Bus** *(Transit Hub at Dusk)*: Recognizing invasive stranger proximity, refusal, positioning near illuminated zones, and boarding near the driver.
2. **A2 — The Cab Ride** *(Verified Transport)*: Checking plates, driver verification, trip sharing, sitting in the rear seat, and route deviation response.
3. **A3 — The Suspicious Chat** *(Digital Phishing & Social Media)*: Recognizing manipulation, refusing OTPs/private meets, reporting to 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in.
4. **A4 — The Crowded Market** *(Personal Space & Active Bystander)*: Disengaging from inappropriate touch, calling for assistance, and finding commercial allies.
5. **A5 — The Late-Night Walk** *(Street Awareness & Havens)*: Footstep cadence detection, crossing streets to test intent, redirecting toward open pharmacies.
6. **A6 — Fake Job Offer** *(Employment Scams)*: Spotting fake recruiters demanding fees or original certificates, verification via MCA portal.
7. **A7 — The Persistent Stalker** *(Clear Boundaries & BNS/IPC Law)*: Refusing isolated meetings, documenting timelines, leveraging campus ICC and Section 354D / 78 BNS.
8. **A8 — The Campus Party** *(Social Gatherings & Vigilance)*: Unattended drink safety, buddy system execution, avoiding drink tampering.
9. **A9 — The Blackmail Call (Sextortion)** *(Cyber Extortion Defense)*: Zero ransom compliance, evidence preservation, filing takedowns via StopNCII.org and 1930.

### Part B: Be a Helper (Relationships, Life Decisions & Legal Autonomy)
1. **B1 — The Betrayal** *(Unsettling Changes & Digital Surveillance)*: Confronting infidelity without self-blame, identifying phone inspections and social isolation as coercive control.
2. **B2 — Who's in Control?** *(Family & In-Law Economic Pressure)*: Resisting ultimatums to surrender careers or passbooks, understanding PWDVA Section 3.
3. **B3 — After the Argument** *(The Controlling Husband & De-escalation)*: Recognizing explosive rage and tracking spyware, preparing an emergency "Go-Bag" (documents, keys, cash).
4. **B4 — Adjust Karo** *(Breaking Point & Lifelines)*: Reaching Sakhi One Stop Centres, obtaining interim Protection Orders and free legal aid via DLSA.
5. **B5 — The Heir** *(Reproductive Coercion & Bodily Autonomy)*: In-law pressure for male children, legal protections under the PC-PNDT Act 1994, reproductive consent.
6. **B6 — Money Matters** *(Economic Abuse & Financial Sovereignty)*: Streedhan recovery under Supreme Court precedents, independent banking, and credit score audits.

## 🎨 Unified Comic Dialogue System

Every mission and episode utilizes the exact same **comic-book visual novel engine**:
* **Speech Bubbles**: Vector ink outlines with directional tails pointing at the active speaker.
* **Thought Clouds**: Scalloped clouds with floating bubbles revealing internal intuition, feelings, and tactical reasoning.
* **Shouts & Whispers**: Jagged impact bursts for confrontations, dashed strokes for quiet asides, and tech frames for phone calls.
* **14 Dynamic Expressions**: Characters expressively blush, laugh, gasp, scowl, and react with manga emotion marks (`GASP!`, `SCAM!`, `STOP!`, `THUMP THUMP!`).
* **Web Audio Procedural Sound Engine**: Synthesizes heartbeats, chimes, party poppers, error buzzers, and footsteps with zero external asset bloat.

## 👤 Dynamic Character Naming (Section 0.5)

No character identity is hardcoded. Players dynamically personalize the story:
* `{hero}`: The Protagonist (Confident, friendly, smart, brave — purple hoodie).
* `{helpedWoman}`: The Woman You Help in Part B (Warm, gentle, kind — saree/kurta).
* `{friendBold}`, `{friendKind}`, `{friendLively}`, `{friendSweet}`: The four supporting allies.
* Change names at any time via **Settings → Character Names**.

## 🛡️ Real Help & Legal Lifelines

Direct tap-to-call integration:
* **112**: Emergency Response Support System (ERSS - Police, Medical, Fire)
* **181**: Women Helpline (Sakhi) 24/7 Crisis Support
* **1091**: Women Police Helpline
* **1930**: National Cyber Crime & Extortion Helpline (cybercrime.gov.in)
* **7827170170**: National Commission for Women (NCW) WhatsApp Emergency Line
* **Sakhi One Stop Centres**: Integrated crisis care in over 700 districts
