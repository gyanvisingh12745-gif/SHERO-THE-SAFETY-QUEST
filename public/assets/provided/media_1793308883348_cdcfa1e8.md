## 3. MISSION REGISTRY (ALL of these must exist, be playable, and appear in the hub)

Each mission has: a title card, a 3-panel animated comic intro, a briefing, 2 to 3 question moments, consequences, "Why?" explanations, a safety tip, and a result panel. Each question has **3 or 4 choices** (Best / Okay but incomplete / Unsafe, plus an optional "looks safe but is a trap"), **shuffled every play**. Write all dialogue, choices and explanations in **English and Hindi**.

### Part A: Street Smarts
1. **A1 The Last Bus (bus stop, night):** a friendly stranger offers a ride, then becomes pushy; the bus arrives. Teach: decline clearly, stay in a lit public place, call a trusted person, say it loudly, share live location and bus number, board near the driver.
2. **A2 The Cab Ride:** verify plate and driver, share the trip, sit in the back, share no personal info, what to do if the route changes.
3. **A3 The Suspicious Chat:** an online "friend" asks for photos, address, OTP, a meeting. Teach: never share these, block and report, save screenshots, tell a trusted adult, Cyber Crime Helpline **1930**, cybercrime.gov.in.
4. **A4 The Crowded Market:** someone follows or touches inappropriately. Teach: move to shopkeepers and families, speak loudly, point at a person and ask for specific help, note a description.
5. **A5 The Late-Night Walk:** a car slows down. Teach: stay on the lit main road, change direction, stay on a phone call, enter a shop or guard post, use SOS.
6. **A6 Fake Job Offer:** high pay, upfront fee, "private interview" at a hotel. Teach: verify the company, never pay to get a job, interview only in official or public places, tell someone, report.
7. **A7 The Stalker:** an ex keeps messaging and shows up. Teach: one clear "no" then stop replying, save evidence, tell trusted people and the college, block, report, avoid being alone.
8. **A8 The Party:** watch your drink, buddy system, check-in time, trust your gut, leave if uncomfortable, help a friend who looks unwell.
9. **A9 The Blackmail Call (sextortion):** a threat to leak private images unless she pays. Teach: do not pay, do not delete evidence, tell a trusted adult, report to 1930, the shame belongs to the blackmailer.

### Part B: Be a Helper (careful, non-graphic; see tone rules below)
Each episode has **two MCQs**:
- **Reality Check (4 options):** (1) Normal disagreement, (2) Unhealthy but not abuse, (3) Abuse: emotional / controlling, (4) Serious abuse: danger now. Add a "Warning Signs vs Healthy Signs" card.
- **What can Anjali safely do? (4 options, shuffled):** e.g. talk to a trusted person, document what is happening, call a helpline, make a safety plan, counselling, versus confronting angrily, suffering silently "for the family", threatening back. Include "try to fix it alone" without shaming, and explain the risks.

1. **B1 The Betrayal (husband cheating):** Anjali discovers Rohan's affair. He mocks her and says it is her fault. 2. **B2 Who's in Control?:** phone checking, money control, isolation from family and friends, "you can't live without me". Teach coercive control and isolation.
3. **B3 After the Argument:** shouting becomes a push and threats; next day he apologises with gifts. **Part B tone rules (mandatory):**

## 6. PERFORMANCE (lag-free is a hard requirement)

- Keep **game state outside React.** React is only for UI overlays and updates **on events, not every frame**. The world is drawn on a single Canvas with a fixed-timestep loop (60 FPS, clamped delta time) that pauses when the tab is hidden.
- Pre-render parallax layers to **offscreen canvases** once, then only translate them. Use **sprite atlases**, reuse textures, use **object pooling** for particles and bubbles, and avoid allocations inside the loop.
- Animate UI with **CSS `transform` and `opacity`** only. Avoid large `backdrop-filter`, big blur filters and heavy box-shadows; fake depth blur by pre-blurring the far background layers in the asset or once in an offscreen canvas.
- **Code-split per mission**, lazy-load assets, and **preload only the next scene**. Free the previous scene's assets on exit.
- Budgets: first load under 3 s on a mid-range phone on 4G; initial JS under 250 KB gzipped; 60 FPS on desktop and at least 30 FPS on low-end phones.
- **Adaptive quality:** measure frame time, and automatically step down (fewer particles, fewer parallax layers, simpler effects) when it rises. Add a Settings option "Quality: Auto / High / Low".
- Add a hidden **FPS and memory overlay** (`?debug=1`). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Stress test:** open and exit missions at least 50 times in a row, and confirm the frame rate and memory stay stable (no growth).
