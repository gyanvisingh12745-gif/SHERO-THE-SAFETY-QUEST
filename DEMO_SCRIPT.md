# SHERO: The Safety Quest — 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

This script provides an engaging, high-impact 3-minute walkthrough of **SHERO: The Safety Quest** for hackathon judges and evaluators.

---

## ⏱️ Timeline Breakdown

| Time | Segment | Key Action & Talking Points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 - 0:30** | **The Hook & Problem** | - Open on `http://localhost:5173`.<br>- Introduce the core challenge: Young women face daily street safety risks, cyber extortion, and subtle relationship coercion without engaging educational tools.<br>- Highlight the solution: A lag-free, full-screen anime comic adventure that teaches real situational awareness and Indian statutory legal rights. |
| **0:30 - 1:00** | **Naming & Part A (Street Smarts)** | - Click **PLAY NOW**.<br>- Show the **"Name Your Hero"** modal: demonstrate typing a name (e.g. *Gyanvi* or *Aira*).<br>- Launch **Mission A1 (The Last Bus)**.<br>- Point out the 3-panel comic intro, high-definition SVG city background, and the **ComicDialogue** system: speech bubble with directional tail and thought cloud displaying inner intuition. |
| **1:00 - 1:45** | **Interactive Choice & Feedback** | - Arrive at the decision point with 3 functional choices.<br>- Choose the correct safe action: highlight the **confetti explosion**, celebratory chime, and verbal feedback: *"Yes, you're correct! You can proceed."*<br>- Show the key takeaways card with safety principles and badge earned. |
| **1:45 - 2:30** | **Part B: Be a Helper & Life Decisions** | - Open the **Mission Directory** (`/missions`).<br>- Switch to **Part B: Relationships & Life Crossroads**.<br>- Show the **"Name the Woman You Help"** screen (*Anjali Didi*).<br>- Launch **Episode B1 (The Betrayal)** or **B5 (The Heir)**.<br>- Demonstrate the permanent **Quick Exit** button (swaps instantly to a neutral weather portal) ensuring player privacy.<br>- Highlight legal literacy: PWDVA 2005, PC-PNDT Act, and 1930 Cyber Helpline. |
| **2:30 - 3:00** | **Developer QA & Real Help Toolkit** | - Open `/debug/scenes` and `/debug/missions`: show all 15 missions running with zero missing data.<br>- Open **Real Help / SafeCircle**: demonstrate direct tap-to-call emergency numbers (112, 1091, 181, 1930, 7827170170).<br>- Conclude with the core motto: **"Be Aware • Be Safe • Be SHERO."** |
