# SHERO: The Safety Quest — Product Pitch & Hackathon Brief

> **"Every choice shapes your journey. Every boundary protects your future."**

---

## 🌟 The Vision
Safety education has historically been boring, passive, or fear-driven. **SHERO: The Safety Quest** transforms women's personal safety and legal literacy into an immersive, emotionally resonant **anime comic adventure game**.

By combining authentic Indian urban environments, interactive comic-book dialogue (speech bubbles + thought clouds), and real-world legal safeguards, SHERO empowers young women to trust their intuition, establish unshakable boundaries, and support loved ones in distress.

---

## 💡 The Core Problem
1. **Unpreparedness in Crisis**: In sudden street harassment, cyber extortion, or predatory rideshares, panic often paralyzes situational awareness.
2. **Invisible Coercive Control**: Women facing relationship manipulation, financial extortion, or family reproductive pressure often struggle to recognize abuse early.
3. **Legal Literacy Gap**: Millions of women are unaware that Indian law guarantees 100% free legal representation (DLSA) and criminalizes digital extortion (1930) and domestic economic deprivation (PWDVA).

---

## 🚀 The SHERO Solution

### 1. Two Connected Sagas (15 Full Missions)
* **Part A: Street Smarts (Missions A1 to A9)**:
  * Public transit, night walks, fake recruitment scams, drink tampering, stalking, and cyber extortion.
  * Gamified with 3 Hearts, 3 Stars, and a dynamic **Safety Meter**.
* **Part B: Be a Helper (Episodes B1 to B6)**:
  * Guiding an adult woman through subtle relationship red flags, coercive control, reproductive pressure, in-law bullying, and Streedhan recovery.
  * Powered by an **Awareness Meter**, supportive retries, and a permanent **Quick Exit** panic button.

### 2. Premium Comic-Book Aesthetics
* **Unified Comic Dialogue**: Custom SVG speech bubbles with directional tails and scalloped thought clouds for inner feelings.
* **14 Emotional Expressions & Lip-Sync**: Expressive anime characters that react in real time with manga marks (`GASP!`, `SCAM!`, `STOP!`, sweat drops, anger veins).
* **Multi-Layered Vector Environments**: High-definition, lag-free backgrounds scaling across `100dvh` / `100dvw`.

### 3. Real-World Lifeline Integration
* Instant tap-to-call direct dials: **112** (ERSS), **1091** (Police), **181** (Women Helpline), **1930** (Cyber Extortion), and **7827170170** (NCW WhatsApp).
* Simple legal cards breaking down PWDVA 2005, PC-PNDT Act, and BNS cruelty statutes.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Architecture & Zero-Lag Engineering
* **Frontend**: React + TypeScript + Vite.
* **Audio**: Procedural Web Audio synthesizer (zero external audio file dependencies) + Web Speech API TTS.
* **Design**: 100% responsive, high-DPI canvas/SVG scaling, zero frame drops.
* **Diagnostics**: Built-in developer routes `/debug/scenes` and `/debug/missions` for comprehensive QA.

---

## 📈 Social Impact & Scalability
SHERO is completely client-side capable, lightweight, and offline-ready. It can be distributed across college campuses, NGO empowerment workshops, and corporate POSH training programs with zero marginal infrastructure cost.
